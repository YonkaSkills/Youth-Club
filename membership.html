Club By-Laws

Explore Membership Tiers

Our club offers structured membership categories defined by the Memorandum of Association and Executive Body regulations.

Founder Category

Donor Member

Founder Members & Signatories

Club By-Law Provision

All those members who have signed on the Memorandum of Association shall be the founder members of the Club.

  • Permanent founder status and club recognition
  • Voting rights in the General Body meetings
  • Complete access to all club facilities & sports amenities
  • Privileged guest room and dining reservations
Category: Founder Tier Inquire with Secretariat
Lifetime Privileges

Life Member

Permanent Individual Membership

Club By-Law Provision

The membership in this category remains during the lifetime of the member.

  • Lifelong, uninterrupted access to all sports and amenities
  • Family privileges for spouse and dependent children
  • Member rates on banquet, dining, and deluxe guest rooms
  • Eligibility to participate in club tournaments and cultural programs
Category: Lifetime Tier Inquire with Secretariat
Executive Induction

Honorary Membership

Chief Patrons & Distinguished Personalities

Club By-Law Provision

The Executive Body may induct any person as Chief Patron. However he will remain as honorary member of the club. His membership is restricted only to those with distinction and whose services are considered necessary to promote the interest of the club.

  • Conferred by resolution of the Executive Body
  • Chief Patron status & guest of honor at club events
  • Full VIP privileges across all association facilities
  • Advisory involvement in promoting club interests
Category: Distinction Tier Inquire with Secretariat
Short-Term Pass

Temporary Membership

Seasonal & Short-Term Enrolment

Club By-Law Provision

Any person may be enrolled as temporary member of the Club for a period not exceeding 3 months in any year.

  • Flexible access valid for up to 3 months per year
  • Ideal for visiting professionals, NRI guests & temporary residents
  • Access to sports facilities, gymnasium & restaurant dining
  • Easy application and quick approval process
Validity: Max 3 Months / Year Apply for Pass
