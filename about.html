Established in 1990, Youth Cultural Association has grown into the region's most beloved community club — offering sports, culture, dining, and fellowship to over 1,000 members.

Our Story

Over 35 Years of Community & Excellence

Youth Cultural Association was established in 1990 with a membership of about 150 in a temporary accommodation. Today the club has around 1,100 members and is a premier recreational hub in Bhimavaram — providing world-class sports facilities, luxury guest rooms, fine dining, and vibrant community events.

We offer a range of activities including All India Ranking Tennis Tournament, Skipping Tournament, Cultural Dance Performances, and much more. We are dedicated to providing our members with opportunities to learn, grow, and connect.
